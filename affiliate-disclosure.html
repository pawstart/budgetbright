Current status — no affiliate links yet

As of this draft, BudgetBright has no affiliate links on any article and no affiliate programme has been approved. No product, tool, app, or service is recommended for commission in any article on this site today.

Adding a fake, placeholder, or unapproved affiliate link would be misleading, so none have been added.

How future affiliate links will be handled, if any are added

  • Any article containing an affiliate link will say so clearly at the top of that article and next to the link.
  • A link will only be added if the product or tool is genuinely explained in that article, and the article does not depend on the link to make sense.
  • Clicking an affiliate link may earn BudgetBright a commission at no extra cost to the reader. That will be stated plainly, without hiding it in small print.
  • No affiliate relationship will change whether a method, template, or habit is recommended. Educational content comes first.

What this page does not do

This page is not a recommendation of any product, and it is not a promise that affiliate income will follow. It exists so that, if affiliate links are ever added after proper approval, readers can find the disclosure in one place.

Questions about this disclosure can be raised via the contact details to be added at launch.
